# Smuggler NPC (Контрабандист)

**Role:** Smuggler, rogue trader contact, criminal underworld
**Voice type:** Male, smooth, sly, street-smart, occasionally nervous

**Status:** 🔍 Needs casting

## Candidate Voice Actors

### 1. John de Lancie
**Known for:** Q (Star Trek), Discord (My Little Pony)
**Rationale:** His Q voice — smooth, smug, theatrical — is perfect for a smuggler who always thinks he's the smartest guy in the room. Has that "I can get you anything, for a price" energy naturally.

### 2. Crispin Freeman
**Known for:** Alucard (Hellsing), various anime/smutty characters
**Rationale:** Excellent for sly, manipulative characters with a sense of humour. Can do street-smart, sleazy, or dangerously charming. His Alucard laugh would work for a smuggler's smugness.

### 3. Jason Marsden
**Known for:** Max Goof, Kovu, various anime dubs
**Rationale:** Excellent "fast talker" voice. Can convey charm, urgency, and dishonesty in equal measure. Good for "I've got a deal for you, captain" type dialogue.

### 4. Matt Mercer (smooth register)
**Known for:** McCree/Cassidy (Overwatch), various D&D NPCs
**Rationale:** His casual, conversational register is perfect for a smuggler who's done this a thousand times. Can do charming rogue or actually-very-dangerous with subtle shifts.
