# Narrator (Рассказчик)

**Role:** The game narrator — reads descriptions, sets the scene

**Status:** 🔍 Needs casting (no narrator DX_Ask bank)

**Voice type:** Deep, authoritative, calm, grimdark — must last for hours without fatigue

**Key requirement:** The English narrator must sound like the voice of the Imperium. Deep, resonant, slightly weary but never emotional. Think "BBC documentary about the end of the world."

## Candidate Voice Actors

### 1. Jonathan Keeble
**Known for:** Warhammer 40K audiobooks (narrator of Horus Heresy, Gaunt's Ghosts, etc.)
**Rationale:** THE voice of Warhammer 40K audiobooks. If there is one perfect voice for 40K narration, it's Jonathan Keeble. Deep, authoritative, handles grimdark with appropriate gravitas. Available on YouTube/audible — easy to find samples.

### 2. Toby Longworth
**Known for:** Warhammer 40K audiobooks (Eisenhorn, Ravenor, many others)
**Rationale:** Another legendary 40K narrator. Slightly more measured than Keeble, excellent at building atmosphere. Huge catalogue of 40K work on audible.

### 3. Jonathan Oliver
**Known for:** Radio drama, documentary narration, Warhammer 40K
**Rationale:** Classic deep British voice. Excellent for authoritative, slightly detached narration. Good for "the voice of the Imperium" style.

### 4. Andrew Wincott
**Known for:** Warhammer 40K audiobooks (various)
**Rationale:** Deep, rich voice with an almost musical quality. Excellent for descriptive passages. Audiobooks readily available for sample extraction.
