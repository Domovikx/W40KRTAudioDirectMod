# Kunrad Voigtvir (Кунрад Войгтвир)

**Role:** Master of Whispers, antagonist
**Voice type:** Young, ambitious, sly, slightly unhinged

**Status:** 🔍 Needs casting (no DX_Ask_Kunrad.bnk in game files)

## Casting Notes

Kunrad is a younger character with a theatrical, manipulative quality. His voice should have:
- Youthful energy with an edge of menace
- Slight theatricality (he enjoys his own performance)
- Moments of genuine madness breaking through

## Candidate Voice Actors

### 1. Jesse Pimentel
**Known for:** Goro Akechi (Persona 5 Royal English dub)
**Rationale:** Captures the "charming psychopath" energy perfectly. Can switch between polite deference and barely concealed contempt in a single sentence.

### 2. Robbie Daymond
**Known for:** Prompto (FFXV), Akechi (Persona 5 vanilla), Tuxedo Mask (Sailor Moon)
**Rationale:** Excellent at playing characters who are performative, smug, and hiding darker motives. Natural tenor range fits Kunrad's age.

### 3. Mark Ota
**Known for:** Various anime/video game roles (Fire Emblem: Three Houses)
**Rationale:** Can deliver the "polite menace" tone. Good at controlled anger and manipulative charm.

### 4. Matt Shipman
**Known for:** Hubert von Vestra (Fire Emblem: Three Houses), various anime roles
**Rationale:** Specialises in sinister, calculating characters with a dark sense of humour. Good fit for Kunrad's "loyal advisor who's clearly up to something" energy.
