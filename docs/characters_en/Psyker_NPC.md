# Psyker NPC (Псайкер)

**Role:** Unsanctioned/sanctioned psyker, navigator, witch
**Voice type:** Male/female, unstable, strained, otherworldly, whispering or booming

**Status:** 🔍 Needs casting

## Candidate Voice Actors

### 1. Michael Matus
**Known for:** The Emperor of Mankind (Horus Heresy audiobooks), various 40K
**Rationale:** Can produce deeply unsettling, resonant vocal effects. His Emperor voice has the right combination of power and instability for a psyker whose grip on sanity is uncertain.

### 2. Emma Gregory
**Known for:** Sister of Silence (Horus Heresy audiobooks), various 40K female characters
**Rationale:** Can deliver cold, inhuman, telepathic-sounding performances. Her Sisters of Silence narration has an ethereal quality perfect for psyker NPCs. Audiobook samples on audible.

### 3. David Tennant (unhinged register)
**Known for:** Scrooge McDuck, Kilgrave (Jessica Jones), various Doctor Who
**Rationale:** When Tennant goes unhinged, it's genuinely disturbing. His Kilgrave in particular — soft-spoken, polite, then casually horrifying — is an excellent template for a dangerous psyker.

### 4. Cate Blanchett (voice work)
**Known for:** Galadriel (LOTR), Hela (Thor Ragnarok)
**Rationale:** Her "voice of the supernatural" is peerless. Could deliver the eerie, distant quality of a psyker touched by the Warp. Especially good for navigator characters who speak in riddles.
